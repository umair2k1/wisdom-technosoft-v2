Privacy

Privacy notice.

Wisdom Technosoft (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what happens to the information you share through wisdomtechnosoft.com and our contact form. It does not cover how we handle client project data once an engagement begins — that is governed by the specific agreement between us and the client.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number, chosen service and your message. These are used only to respond to your inquiry.
  • Email and phone: if you write to wisdomtechnosoft04@gmail.com or call +91 74473 31127, your message and reply history are retained for correspondence.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our hosting provider records standard request data (IP address, timestamp, requested URL, user-agent) for security and reliability. These are not used for advertising or analytics.

What we do not do

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No third-party analytics scripts (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc.).
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with third parties for marketing.
  • No automated decision-making based on your inquiry.

How long we keep it

Contact-form submissions and correspondence are kept for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, if a project begins, for the duration of that project and any statutory record-keeping requirement. You may ask us to delete your inquiry at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you have sent us by writing to wisdomtechnosoft04@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and no later than 30 days.

Security

The website is served over HTTPS. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that affects how we handle your information, we will update the date below and, where appropriate, notify you.
